Rummitimer — Turn Timer for Rummikub & Board Games

Tired of endless "just one more second..." moments during Rummikub, Janggi, Go, or Chess?
Rummitimer keeps every player honest and the game moving — now with dedicated clocks for four board games, plus a set of general-purpose timers, all in one app.

---------------------------------------
🎲 Board Game Timers
---------------------------------------

• Rummikub Timer
Choose 2, 3, or 4 players and a per-turn time limit (30/60/90/120 seconds). A metronome tick sounds every 10 seconds, then every second in the final 10 — and pressing Next auto-starts the following player's turn after a 1-second delay. When time runs out, log the penalty and keep playing; enter each player's leftover tile score at the end to see the final ranking.

• Janggi Timer
A simple countdown clock for each player — pick your time limit and go.

• Baduk (Go) Timer
Main time plus byoyomi (countdown periods) — configure main time, per-period seconds, and number of periods, with a visual ring showing periods remaining.

• Chess Timer
Main time with increment — each move adds seconds back to your clock, Fischer-style.

All three head-to-head clocks share a tap-your-side-to-pass interface, with the active player's clock highlighted and the game result (time used per player) shown at the end.
